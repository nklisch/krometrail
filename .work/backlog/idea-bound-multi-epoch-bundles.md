A compact temporal bundle can fan out dramatically across short-lived visual epochs. After one batch changed viewport through responsive tablet, responsive desktop, mobile phone, custom 512x768, and clear, a `latest_interaction` bundle with a one-second window resolved five epochs and 13 frames. Compact mode generated 15 artifacts and exposed 30 artifact/manifest resource entries; four epochs contained only one source frame but each still produced storyboard, before/during/after, and difference-map outcomes. The evidence was truthful and capture remained healthy, but the supposedly compact result was much larger than the question and produced several low-information single-frame artifacts.
