A concise temporal bundle and its `range_handle` browser-event follow-up serialized every full source-frame UUID in the resolved range. A 3.1-second, 29-frame interaction already carried 29 identifiers in each response, dominating the otherwise compact output and scaling linearly with capture cadence and interval length. The exact ordered IDs remain important provenance behind deliberate detail or manifest reads, but the default agent surface should stay bounded while retaining range identity, counts, gaps, and drill-down authority.
